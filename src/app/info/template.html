THE 5 MISTAKES

These quietly deleted eleven months of my progress. I’d bet money you’re making at least two of them right now.

  1. 1

    Program hopping

    Every program got three weeks. The first weeks are mostly your nervous system learning the movement. The muscle comes after. I kept restarting right before the good part.

    The fix One plan. Twelve weeks minimum before you’re allowed to judge it.

  2. 2

    No progressive overload

    Same dumbbells, same reps, every week. I got a pump, so I assumed it was working. The pump is a liar. Your body goes, “Cool, we can already do this,” and builds nothing new.

    The fix Hit the top of your rep range on every set, then add a little weight. Boring. It works.

  3. 3

    Eating “clean,” but not eating enough

    Chicken, rice, salad. I was eating like a very disciplined small bird, and I never counted.

    ~1.6 gprotein per kg of bodyweight per day is where muscle gains level off.Morton et al., 2018, a review of 49 studies

    The fix Count for a few weeks, long enough to learn what your protein target actually looks like on a plate.

  4. 4

    Training everything the same

    My shoulders, chest and arms were exactly what made me look like I didn’t lift, and I trained them exactly like everything else. I couldn’t see it because of the mirror lie: same mirror, same angle, every day. Your brain quietly updates the picture, so today always looks like yesterday.

    The fix Compare photos side by side. Give your weak points extra work.

  5. 5

    Sleeping like a raccoon

    Phone in bed. Five, maybe six hours. You don’t grow in the gym. The gym breaks the muscle down. You grow while you sleep.

    −18%muscle protein synthesis after one night without sleep.Lamon et al., 2021

    The fix Phone out of the bedroom. Same bedtime. Seven to eight hours.

I wasn’t lazy. I trained hard. I just had five small leaks that cancelled out everything I poured in.

And nobody told me, because nobody was looking.
