THE 5 MISTAKES

  1. 1

    Program hopping

    I kept restarting before I could build on anything.

    The fix: Give one plan at least twelve weeks.

  2. 2

    No progressive overload

    Same weights, same reps: no new reason to adapt.

    The fix: Reach the top of your rep range, then add weight.

  3. 3

    Eating “clean,” but not eating enough

    Too little protein and food to build much.

    The fix: Track long enough to learn your calorie and protein targets.

  4. 4

    Training everything the same

    My shoulders, chest, and arms needed more attention.

    The fix: Compare photos. Give weak points extra work.

  5. 5

    Sleeping like a raccoon

    Hard training. Five hours to recover.

    The fix: Phone out of the bedroom. Seven to eight hours of sleep.

What worked

My next year, on one page.

  • Training. Push, pull, legs, upper, lower. About an hour. Compounds: 5–8 reps; smaller muscles: 10–15. Stop 1–2 reps short of failure; add weight at the top of the range. Extra work for weak points.
  • Food. Around 0.7 g protein per pound daily. Lean bulk, then a cut. Ordinary food, eaten consistently.
  • Sleep. Phone in another room. Same bedtime. Seven to eight hours.
  • The loop. Sunday photos and weight. Change one thing. This was my routine; yours may differ.
